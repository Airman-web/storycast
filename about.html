About StoryCast

Stories that belong to everyone.

An accessible audio and video platform built around aviation. Every story is designed to be heard, read, or navigated by anyone regardless of ability, device, or connection speed.

Our Mission

We believe aviation stories deserve to reach every listener. That means building for screen readers, keyboards, low-bandwidth connections, and every device in between. Accessibility is not a feature we added at the end — it is the foundation the entire platform was built on.

Accessibility Features

Captions and Transcripts

Every audio and video story includes full closed captions delivered via WebVTT and a complete written transcript available inline on the page or as a plain-text download. No story requires working audio to follow.

Keyboard Navigation

All interactive elements links, buttons, the audio player, and navigation are fully reachable and operable using a keyboard alone. Tab order follows a logical left to right, top to bottom reading sequence on every page.

Colour Contrast WCAG AA

All text on content sections meets a minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio against its background. Hero text on the warm greige gradient meets 4.5:1 against the darkest point of the gradient. All ratios have been independently verified.

Screen Reader Support

All pages use semantic HTML5 elements including header, main, nav, section, article, aside, figure, and footer so screen readers can navigate by landmark. ARIA labels are added only where semantic HTML is not sufficient on its own.

How to Engage

Listen

Stream any story directly in your browser using the native HTML audio player. No account needed, no app to download, and no plugin required on any device or operating system.

Read

Every story has a full transcript printed below the player on the story page. Read at your own pace with no time pressure, or download the transcript as a plain-text file to read offline.

Share

Each story has its own permanent URL. Copy the address bar and share it directly. The person on the receiving end does not need an account or any software to access the story.
