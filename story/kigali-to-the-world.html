00:00 — A New Gateway

The morning sun rises over the hills of Bugesera. Below the horizon, cranes, runways, and terminal structures are becoming a new gateway to Rwanda. This is Bugesera International Airport — a modern aviation hub designed to welcome travellers, connect businesses, and carry Rwandan stories into the world.

00:21 — Why Bugesera Is Being Built

For years, Kigali International Airport has served as Rwanda’s doorway to the globe. But as passenger numbers grow, airlines expand, and national ambitions rise, a larger and more efficient facility is needed. Bugesera is planned to handle bigger aircraft, increasing traffic, and future expansion, while improving the journey from arrival to departure.

00:44 — Completed Terminal Design

When completed, the terminal presents a calm, contemporary face: broad drop-off zones, clean lines, glass façades, shaded roofs, and clear multilingual signage. Inside, daylight fills the check-in hall, reducing harsh shadows and making the space feel open. Wide walkways, accessible counters, tactile guidance paths, clear signage, and audible announcements help every passenger move with confidence.

01:10 — Departure Passenger Journey

A departing traveller steps from a taxi beneath the canopy and enters a hall designed for clarity. Self-service kiosks offer a quick option for passengers without checked bags, while staffed counters support families, groups, and travellers who need assistance. After check-in, luggage moves along conveyors into sorting systems. Passengers continue through security screening, then immigration, where separate lanes can manage residents, visitors, transit passengers, and crew. Beyond the formalities, the terminal opens into departure lounges with shops, cafés, charging points, quiet seating, restrooms, and gates placed along natural walking routes.

01:50 — Arrival Passenger Journey

Arrivals follow an equally guided path. After the aircraft parks, passengers move through the jet bridge into the terminal. International travellers first reach immigration, where officers verify passports and entry documents. Then they proceed to baggage reclaim, where large carousels and real-time flight displays make it easier to identify the correct belt. After collecting luggage, passengers pass through customs and enter the arrivals hall, where taxis, shuttles, ride-hailing bays, rental cars, and public transport links await.

02:24 — Aircraft Landing and Airside Movement

Outside, the runway gives the airport its rhythm. An aircraft approaches through the East African sky, guided by air traffic control onto final alignment. Landing gear lowers, flaps extend, and the plane touches down smoothly on the centre line. Spoilers and thrust reversers slow it, while runway markings and edge lights keep the path clear. The aircraft then exits to a taxiway and moves toward its assigned stand, where ground crews, fuel trucks, catering vans, power units, and baggage tractors prepare it for the next journey.

03:02 — Cargo and Behind-the-Scenes Operations

But Bugesera is more than passenger halls and runways. Cargo facilities allow Rwandan products — coffee, tea, flowers, pharmaceuticals, and manufactured goods — to reach international markets quickly. Fuel farms, maintenance areas, fire stations, and control towers operate behind the scenes, keeping the airport safe, secure, and reliable.

03:23 — Rwanda’s Gateway to the World

Connected by roads and future transport links to Kigali and the wider region, Bugesera is built to support tourism, trade, and regional integration. When the terminal glows at dusk and planes continue to land, Rwanda’s new gateway sends a clear message: the country is ready to meet the world.
